## Explicitly rejected or unestablished assertions

- Universal −30 to −45 °C flash-point range: contradicted by NIOSH's ethyl-formate entry (about 54 °C boiling and −20 °C flash point); this correction does not make that fluid acceptable.
- Universal infeasibility of the entire proposed chemical space: not established by the supplied examples. A bounded, sourced search and explicit fire requirement are needed.
- Small integer outcomes can never yield a useful interval, and several hundred substances are always required: not a valid general statistical rule.
- A zero baseline was unhandled or profile changes were forbidden: v1.0 already allowed an absolute-gain rule and explicitly versioned profile changes.
- Every documentation task should be Tier 1 or every credible review must reject findings: neither follows. Consequence, evidence and independence govern the assessment.
- A generic public strategy automatically destroys novelty of later undisclosed formulations: not established; claim/disclosure-specific professional advice is required.
