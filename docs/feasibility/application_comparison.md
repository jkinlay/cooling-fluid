## Three distinct questions

| Profile | Thermal mechanism and fluid-contact boundary | Evidence needed before a candidate judgement | Current decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `TP_IMMERSION_F0` — two-phase immersion | Dielectric fluid contacts immersed IT; evaporation near heat sources and condensation transfer heat. Open bath versus sealed chassis, wetted materials, vapour capture, operating/fault pressure, and electrical interfaces remain system choices. OCP distinguishes two-phase immersion and system arrangements in §§2.1–2.3 [S1]. The proposed buyer is an operator of such a system; the buyer and equipment arrangement are not yet validated. | Accepted temperature/pressure envelope and its pressure basis; normal-boiling versus saturation conditions; fluid charge and loss path; electrical test method; fire scenario and method; materials, SDS, jurisdiction, OEM, insurer. The historical inclusive 45–55 °C **normal-boiling** window and fluorine-free structural policy are proposed search boundaries only, not adopted operating or safety limits [S4]. PFAS-free wording cannot substitute for structural identity. | `RESEARCH_DRAFT`; no numerical hard gate adopted. |
| `SP_IMMERSION` — single-phase immersion | Dielectric liquid contacts immersed IT and circulates through a heat exchanger without intended phase change [S1]. Bath/chassis arrangement, flow and pumping are application details. A buyer with an unmet requirement is a hypothesis, not a validated customer. | Accepted liquid operating/fault temperature and pressure; viscosity and thermal performance under those conditions; electrical/fire margins; ageing and wetted-material compatibility; jurisdiction, OEM, insurer. It cannot inherit the two-phase 45–55 °C boiling window [S4]. | `RESEARCH_DRAFT`; no numerical hard gate adopted. |
| `TP_DIRECT_CHIP` — two-phase direct-to-chip | Fluid is primarily inside a closed technology-cooling-system loop, cold plates, seals and heat exchangers, rather than bathing the whole IT assembly [S2]. A leak/fault creates a separate exposure case. A TCS integrator/operator is a proposed buyer, not a validated one. | Saturation/pressure envelope, liquid/vapour flow and charge, cold-plate and seal material list, leak/fault protection, service conditions, fluid classification, OEM and insurer acceptance. OCP says the design team must determine applicability to the specific project [S2]. Immersion limits are not silently transferable [S4]. | `RESEARCH_DRAFT`; no numerical hard gate adopted. |

ASHRAE distinguishes liquid-cooled racks, liquid-cooled equipment and directly liquid-cooled electronics, and notes that liquid choices are wider than water [S3]. This supports keeping the contact boundary explicit; it does not set a fluid acceptance threshold. OCP's immersion specification includes fluid-property/test-method tables and jurisdiction-dependent equipment certification [S1, §§3.1, 4.1–4.2]; this is a source to assess, not evidence that its values apply automatically to all three profiles. The OCP direct-to-chip guideline states different lower boiling-point bounds in section 3.1 (−26.3 °C as a minimum criterion) and Table 1 (+26.3 °C as a typical property) [S2, p.9]; neither value is adopted here.

## Open owner and equipment decisions

1. Name the **initial application and equipment arrangement** for a first useful offline analysis, or explicitly retain all three in parallel. This choice does not retrospectively change earlier results.
2. Supply or approve application-specific operating and fault temperature/pressure envelopes with gauge/absolute basis, phase and inclusive boundary. Until then numerical screening remains exploratory or UNKNOWN.
3. Identify target jurisdiction(s), applicable standard edition/clause, and the equipment/OEM/insurer whose rules would govern. No generic NFPA assertion or vendor marketing claim is used as a universal exclusion.
4. Define direct fluid-contact and leakage boundaries, materials, electrical test conditions, fire scenario/method and required SDS/environmental evidence for each profile.
5. Decide whether the historical `TP_IMMERSION_F0` 45–55 °C normal-boiling screen is retained only as a search aid or proposed for a specific system. It does not establish commercial deployability.

No unanswered mandatory item can be marked PASS. In particular, a profile with physical properties described but insurer acceptance unresolved remains `RESEARCH_DRAFT`. A `PFAS-free` label alone is not evidence that a substance is fluorine-free; chemical identity and the relevant definition must be checked. An OCP example fluid or manufacturer aspiration is not buyer/OEM approval.
